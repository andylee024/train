# Principle: DNT Olympic Lifts

**What Andy has learned:** he has a great Olympic lifting coach, and his lifts improve when her program is respected. The plan protects her Olympic lifts. Strength work is his own (`principle-dylan-shannon-strength-wave`).

## The rules

1. **Her Olympic lifts, sets and percentages run as written.** We only decide which day each lands on.
2. **Olympic lifts open the session (1a)** on lifting days. Andy likes starting there.
3. **Skip her back squats.** The squat wave covers squatting.
4. **Her clean / snatch deadlifts may replace his hinge** for that week.
5. **Her accessories** (good mornings, pull-ups, dips, wrist work, core, reverse plank) fill block 3 slots.
6. **Never add her days on top of the week.** Her 3 days fold into his split. Bolting a program on top caused his Block 1 fall-off.

## Where her lifts land

Coach slots in the week: **L1 1a (Sun) · U1 1a (Mon) · L2 1a/1b (Thu) · U2 1a (Fri)**.

| Her day type | Lands on | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean & jerk / power clean (+ back squat) | **Sun L1 1a** | Heavy day; her squat is skipped |
| Clean + clean deadlift / pull | **Thu L2 1a/1b** | 48 h before Saturday |
| Snatch (+ snatch deadlift) | **Sun or Thu 1a**, rotated in when her program has one | Keeps heavy pulls off Tue/Fri |
| Jerk / push press / overhead | **Fri U2 1a** (or Mon U1 1a) | Light on the legs |
| Any lighter Olympic lift | **Mon U1 1a** | Upper day, legs fresh for Tue |

Jump-day primers (S1 hang power snatch, J1 power clean) are light: about 4×2 at 60–65%.

## On each new drop (~every 2 weeks)
1. Read the program PDF and save it to the active arc's `coach-programs/` as `dnt-wk<NN-NN>_arc-W<NN>-W<NN>.pdf`. Arc week = DNT week; numbering restarts at W1 after W24.
2. Sort each day by type and place it using the table above.
3. Remove her back squat. Swap her pulls for his hinge. Put her accessories in block 3.
4. Check: 6 or fewer exercises per day; no heavy pulls on Friday; heavy legs at least 48 h before Saturday.
5. Keep her percentages. Convert to lb only from known maxes, otherwise leave the %.
6. Note anything dropped.
7. Align test weeks: ask her to peak around planned max days (currently W10 Thu, Jan 14).
