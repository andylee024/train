# Block P — Power + Approach (W1–W4 · Nov 8 – Dec 5, 2026)

Week number = `(today − Nov 8) ÷ 7 + 1` = DNT coach week. Read **§1 the week** (same every week) and **§2 your week's row** (what changes). Coach lifts come from `../../coach-programs/` and are placed by `principle-dnt-olympic-lifts`.

## Purpose
Learn the approach and make the strength fast. Jumping is the main lift; the Olympic lifts open every session as power work with the coach; the squat is held at two sets to a box; flexibility starts as a skill (hip IR Mon, overhead Fri).

## Goals (binary, scored at the W4 retest)
1. **Baseline captured W1** (ball-ladder rung + hand depth, standing reach, seated hip IR L/R, Taylor's pose cm, supine overhead test, heel-elevated squat test, 40 kg OHS) and **the ladder logged every week** to `measurements`.
2. **One rung or one hand-depth step above baseline at the W4 retest.**
3. **Four core sessions a week, every week** (Sun L1 · Mon U1 · Tue J1 · Thu P1).

## Measurement
**Ball ladder**, once a week at a rim, fresh, before the session (Tue if it has a rim, else Sat — then Sat isn't optional). Rungs: 0 rim grab · 1 tennis ball (≈10'2") · 2 softball (10'3") · 3 mini basketball (10'5") · **4 volleyball (10'6")** · 5 women's ball (10'7") · 6 men's ball (10'8"). Six attempts at the current rung, 2' rest; score clean / touched / missed, how many of six, hand depth on the best jump (fingertips / palm / wrist / forearm); two clean of six → next rung next session. → `measurements`: `ladder_rung` (value = rung, value_text = ball), `ladder_clean_of_6`, `hand_depth`. Bodyweight Sun → `daily_metrics` (189 → 187 by W4).

## Jump drivers (the weekly check)
The Jump Drivers dashboard (claude.ai artifact, reads Supabase live) scores five drivers 0–100 and combines them into one composite (weighted geometric mean): **Reactive** (depth-jump touch − standing touch; 0" → 4"; 25%) · **Explosive** (power clean ÷ back squat; 0.55 → 0.75; 25%) · **Approach skill** (approach touch − standing touch; 3" → 9"; 20%) · **Strength** (squat e1RM ÷ BW; 1.5 → 2.0; 15%) · **Bodyweight** (195 → 184 lb; 15%). Touch, ladder rung and jump sessions are the check, not part of the score. **Goals for Jan 30 = each driver's 100 line:** depth-jump gap +4" · clean ÷ squat 0.75 · approach gap 9" · squat 2.0× BW · 184 lb. Each card shows a straight pace line from the first reading since Nov 8 to that goal: on pace, behind (with the weekly change needed), or goal hit.

**What gets measured, and when (low friction):**
- **Every week, free:** Explosive, Strength and Bodyweight come from lifts already logged and the Sunday weigh-in. The ball ladder is the touch check.
- **Reactive, weekly = dose, not score:** the card counts reactive contacts (logged reps of Depth Jump, Low Box Depth Jump, Pogo Jumps (ankle hops), Single-Leg Pogos, Reactive Hurdle Jumps, Single-Leg Bounds) against ~55 a week. On plan ≥45 · light 1–44 · none 0. So log reps for those.
- **Reactive + Approach scores, at the W4 and W8 retests only:** wall test, best of 3 each, inches from the floor → `measurements`: `standing_touch_in`, `dj_touch_in` (off an 18" box), `approach_touch_in`. The two scores carry forward until the next retest; their status is vs the previous retest.
- Lifts carry forward too: best set in the last 6 weeks, else the last 12.

Weigh in Sundays.

Each Sunday, per driver (status = score now vs 3 weeks ago, or vs the previous retest for Reactive and Approach; ±3 = improving / slipping):
- **The limiter** (lowest driver) gets the most attention next week.
- **Reactive** dose light or none → get J1's depth jumps and hops in next week (no make-ups) · retest gap flat → same box, 3×3 at full intent, 2' rest, add pogos/hurdle hops · slipping → cut contacts by a third, no depth jumps after heavy legs; two weeks down → light week.
- **Explosive** flat → speed, not load: jump squats 20–25% (P) / speed squats 60–65% (R) · slipping → check the clean, not the ratio; end clean sets when the bar slows.
- **Approach skill** flat → 6 filmed 3-step drills before max jumps (long, low penultimate) · slipping → full rests, stop when touch drops twice.
- **Strength** flat → hold; only under 1.85× add a third Sunday squat set · slipping → add a set back for two weeks.
- **Bodyweight** flat or rising two weeks → trim intake on rest days.
- **Composite up, touch flat 3 weeks** → the drivers or weights are wrong: recalibrate.

## Rules
- **Right hip + groin:** physio cleared it (Nov 17): train strength and end range. Depth is pain-guided (≤ 3/10 during, settled by next morning); lower the squat box when the last one is easy. Pain ≥ 3/10 → that movement out for the week. Deep-squat sessions Mon + Fri even before jump days (Andy's call); check: two Tuesdays with lower best hand depth, or groin soreness on a jump day → move Monday's session to Sunday after the squat.
- **Left knee (free leg):** never squat deep cold — 10 box squats + Spanish-squat holds 2×30 s before the first loaded rep on leg and jump days. Pain during a session ≥ 3/10 → no depth jumps that week.
- **Shoulders:** not a current issue; landmine / hang variants only if something returns at ≥ 3/10.
- **Knee ≥ 3/10:** no depth jumps that week.
- **Ladder flat 3 weeks:** Sunday squat → 1 set, +4 approach jumps Thu. **Squat top set −5% two weeks** with the ladder rising → add a set; with the ladder flat → halve Tuesday for a week.
- **Missed day:** if we miss, we miss: skip it and run the current week as written. No make-ups, no repeating last week's loads, never double up. Only exception: lost Sunday → L1 Mon, J1 Wed (keeps the squat wave and the 48 h).
- **Busy week:** keep Tue, Sun, Mon, Thu in that order. Fri/Sat skipped is by design.
