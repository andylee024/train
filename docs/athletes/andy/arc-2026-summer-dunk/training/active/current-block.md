## Purpose

The final block, run on the **v9 Oly-forward split**. The 6-week lower wave (back + front squat + deadlift) builds volume to a crest, then tapers so the **W16 tests land fresh**. The arc has pivoted toward an **Olympic-lifting / strength identity** — the coach's DNT Oly work drives Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat, the athlete's own strength waves anchor Sun/Mon. The Saturday jump/dunk is now **optional** (opportunistic, not the weekly boss), but the dunk is **still tested** at W16 alongside bench, clean/jerk, and pull-up.

## Programming Strategy (v9)

1. **Split (fixed):** Sun Heavy Lower · Mon Heavy Upper · Tue Oly + mobility legs · Wed BJJ-only · Thu Oly + lower (front squat) · Fri Oly + upper *(opt)* · Sat Oly + jump/dunk *(opt)*.
2. **6-week lower wave** (back squat / front squat / deadlift), 2–4 sets, records per variation × rep-scheme: **W11** 3×6 → **W12** 3×8 → **W13** 4×6 → **W14** 4×4 (heavy crest) → **W15** taper → **W16** deload/test. Variation rotates monthly (back: full/pause/pin/half · front: full/pause/tempo).
3. **Upper wave** (bench variation + weighted pull-up) runs its own cycle on Mon, driving the G5 bench + pull-up tests.
4. **Coach DNT Oly first** on Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat (~2/day by pattern: snatch→Tue, clean/jerk→Thu, jerk/press→Fri, power→Sat); coach accessories fold into Thu/Fri; coach back squat + deadlift dropped (own squat waves cover it). **6-exercise cap.** Current drop: **DNT wk11–12 = W11–W12**; next (wk13–14) → W13–W14.
5. **Intensity curve:** W11–12 RPE 7–8 (realize) · W13–14 RPE 8–9 (peak crest) · **W15 TAPER** (~50% volume, 90% intensity) · **W16 TEST WEEK**.
6. **Two heavy squat exposures/week** (Sun back + Thu front) — the higher squat volume is the v9 trade-off vs. the reactivity-deficit diagnosis; watch the daily bw log + Sat jump quality (when run) as canaries.
7. **Nutrition → maintenance** (no cut during peak, VJ §9).
8. **Secondaries taper into W15–W16:** BJJ → 1 light night max in W15; side-split flex → maintenance; everything bows to the W16 tests.
9. **Optional-day rule:** Fri + Sat are optional — on a heavy week drop to the 4 core days (Sun/Mon/Tue/Thu) + Wed BJJ; skipping them is by design, not a fall-off. If Fri is skipped, slide its coach jerk to Sat.
