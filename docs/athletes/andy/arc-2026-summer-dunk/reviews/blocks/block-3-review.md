## 4. What worked

- **The 4-day core spine held all six weeks** — the split's "optional Fri/Sat" hedge worked exactly as intended; adherence never cracked even on lighter weeks.
- **Lower-body waves drove PRs** — undulating 2–4 set waves on back/front squat + deadlift are clearly productive for this athlete.
- **Coach integration was clean** — DNT lifts folded onto Tue/Thu/Fri without breaching the 6-cap or causing the Block-1-style overload.
- **Front squat as the Thursday anchor** carried over well and progressed fastest.

## 5. What didn't / gaps

- **Bodyweight logging collapsed** — 1 entry (189 lb, Jul 30) in 6 weeks. Can't assess the cut/maintenance or use it as a fatigue canary. This was a reliable habit earlier in the arc; it lapsed.
- **Bench under-trained** — only 3 bench sessions logged; several Mondays missing the press. G5 bench 260 needs more consistent exposure.
- **Zero jump/dunk work logged** — no Saturday sessions all block. Under v9, Sat is optional and it got skipped every week, so **G1 (dunk) effectively received no training this block.** The dunk is now genuinely dormant.
- **Side-split (G3) not tracked** — no measurements logged; the Sunday tape-measure habit lapsed.
- **Olympic lifts nowhere near the 225 goal** — kept light by design.

## 6. Carry-forward decisions (keep / change / drop)

- **KEEP:** the v9 lower waves (back/front squat + deadlift) — they're producing. Same undulating structure next block.
- **KEEP:** the 4-day core / optional-Fri-Sat structure — adherence proof it fits his life.
- **CHANGE:** make **bodyweight logging** a hard post-workout step again (it's the fatigue + body-comp canary and it went dark).
- **CHANGE:** if **bench 260** still matters, protect Monday bench — don't let it be the skipped session.
- **DECIDE:** **G1 dunk and G2 clean/jerk 225 got no real progress** this block (dunk untrained, Oly kept light). Either formally deprioritize them for this Oly-strength phase, or carve them a real slot next block. Right now they're goals on paper with no training behind them.

## 7. Recommendations for the next block / arc

1. **Name the identity explicitly.** This block became a strength/Olympic-development phase (squat + front squat + deadlift PRs), not a dunk peak. The next arc should either commit to that (drop dunk/jump from the goal list) or rebuild a jump day back in.
2. **Reinstate the canaries** — daily bw + weekly side-split measure — or stop listing G3 as a tracked goal.
3. **If clean/jerk 225 is real,** it needs its own heavy progression, not just the coach's technique-% work.
4. Feed this review into the next `plan-arc` as the pre-arc problem statement.
