## 4. The gap — reactive / jump / dunk

Searching the whole 3-month log for jump, plyo, depth-jump, approach, bound, or dunk work:

- **Jump Squats:** last done **Jul 2** (pre-v9). Stopped.
- **Depth / box / approach jumps, dunk attempts:** **zero logged** in 3 months.
- **Sprints:** none from late June until **Sep 14** — when the new reactive finisher kicked in.

So for ~10 weeks, the athlete did **no reactive or jump training at all.** Your core diagnosis (VJ §3) is *strength-dominant, reactivity-deficient* — RFD/SSC is the bottleneck for jumping. The block trained the quality you already have (max force) and skipped the one you're missing. That's why "am I improving as an athlete?" splits in two: the barbell says yes, the jump has no data either way.

**Good news:** the reactive finisher (2 plyos + sprints on upper days) started W17 (Sep 14). That's the fix beginning — it just needs to *keep running*.

## 6. What to do next

1. **Own the identity — or rebalance it.** The honest read: this became a strength/Oly block and it's working. Two clean paths:
   - **Keep it strength-first** → drop the dunk from the goal list (or park it), and enjoy that squat/bench/clean are climbing.
   - **Recommit to the dunk** → the reactive finisher (just started) becomes non-negotiable, add a real weekly jump/dunk session, and *measure* it.
   Right now you're implicitly doing the first while the plan still lists the dunk as the boss. Pick.
2. **Keep the reactive finisher alive.** It started Sep 14 — the single highest-leverage change for the athlete goal. Log jump height / sprint times so it's trackable.
3. **Reinstate the bodyweight log** (post-workout, training days). It's the cheapest data point and it's been dark.
4. **Bank real maxes.** Several e1RMs are back-calculated off submaximal work. One honest top single each on squat / bench / clean would calibrate the whole plan (coach % → real lb) and give a clean baseline for the next arc. W18's coach programming already builds toward a power-clean max — use it.

## 7. Bottom line

You're **more of an athlete than 3 months ago in strength and consistency** — meaningfully so, and the consistency is the headline given your history. But you have **not** yet moved the needle on the reactive/jump quality that was the whole point of "the dunk arc," because it wasn't trained. The next arc's job is to decide whether the dunk is still the goal, and if so, to train it on purpose — with the reactive work you just started, plus the measurement to prove it's working.
